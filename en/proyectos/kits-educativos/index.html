A project by Ideo Maker

Bicho-bot educational kits

A teaching robot born for the Lo Barnechea workshops that became an MDF kit, designed to be manufactured and fit in its box.

A project byIdeo Maker
Year2022 — 2023
My roleProduct design and kit graphics
ServicesProduct · STEAM · Digital fabrication · Robotics

This is a project by Ideo Maker, made by its team. Here I show the part I worked on.

Bicho-bot was born in July 2022 as a teaching robot for the workshops of the Lo Barnechea Cultural Corporation, for girls and boys aged 6 to 12. It was my first project at Ideo Maker.

In 2023 we turned it into an Ideo Maker kit, to be sold. It was improved and updated, and the parts and graphics were designed so it could be mass produced and fit inside the box.

01My role

  • Product design
  • Fit and digital fabrication: MDF parts that fit inside the box
  • Graphic design for the kit version, with Patricia Ramírez

Since then it has been the workshop Ideo Maker brings to every Congreso Futuro en tu comuna event, in high schools and hospitals.

02The kit graphics

The graphics for the kit version, the box, the instruction booklet and the cards, were designed by Patricia Ramírez and me.
