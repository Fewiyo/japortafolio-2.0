Tech Farming

An experience about farming and technology for KAOS, by the Mustakis Foundation, built around one arm with three interchangeable tools.

ClientIdeo Maker, for Mustakis Foundation
Year2023
ServicesProduct design · Digital fabrication · 3D printing · Education

Tech Farming is an educational experience about farming and technology that Ideo Maker developed for KAOS, the Mustakis Foundation's creative space. Participants go through four tables and at each one they do a potato-farming task with a mechanical arm: digging the soil, turning it, planting and watering.

My role

  • Design of the initial experience, with Elvis Andrade
  • Design of the robotic claw
  • Fabrication with 3D printing and laser cutting
  • Assistant in some of the workshops

Ideo Maker team

  • Vicente Cáceres
  • Elvis Andrade
  • Patricia Ramírez
  • Sebastián Higuera
  • Javiera Matus

One arm, three tools

The heart of the experience is a single arm whose tool changes with the task: a shovel for the soil, a claw to pick up and plant the potatoes, and a dropper for watering. The claw is the most mechanically complex piece of the set.

The experience

The reference was Overcooked, the video game where a team cooks by splitting up stations. Here the stations are the four farming tables. Each table has six arms, one of each color, and at the end of the route are the planters, arranged by the same colors.

How it changed

The first design was made with Elvis Andrade. Then Patricia Ramírez and Sebastián Higuera improved it, and it kept being refined in the workshops themselves, taught by Patricia Ramírez and Javiera Matus. I was an assistant in some of them.
