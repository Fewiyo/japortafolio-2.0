02 / Services

Everything an institution needs to sustain a maker space.

Service design

Experience mapping, proposal architecture and management models. I turn complex processes into services an institution can actually run.

FabLab creation

From floor plan to first prototype: layout, equipment selection, safety protocols, budget and training for the team that will run it.

Teaching and STEAM

Design of educational programs and kits using the Design Thinking methodology. Classes, workshops and materials for teachers who replicate the model.

Digital fabrication

3D modeling, printing, laser cutting and applied electronics. Working prototypes and teaching pieces ready for classroom use.

Web apps and platforms

Digital tools built from a concrete problem: mental health support, training, household chores or finding a graduate program. Ten projects built with AI, from prototype to something used every day.

Editorial and information design

Publications, guides, instruction booklets and infographics that make technical content readable. From a kit manual to a printed national health plan.

Photography

Documenting projects, processes and activities, for reports, press and portfolios. I have photographed for ECLAC and the Pan American Health Organization.
